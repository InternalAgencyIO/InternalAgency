# Visual bible

## Cast lock

All characters are fictional adults.

- Radiance: tall blonde woman, 25+, poised; the same fully opaque sleeveless
  red couture-latex knee-length dress with a white collar and cobalt/white side
  panels in every scene.
- Ellie: shortest of the quartet, 25+, short deep-brunette bob; the same Paris
  first-officer jacket and knee-length skirt in crisp white with a red belt and
  cobalt/red piping in every scene.
- Ece: petite Turkish-descended brunette, 25+, long dark hair and green eyes;
  the same cobalt double-breasted Paris captain jacket and knee-length skirt
  with gold buttons, a red belt, and red/white piping in every scene.
- Alia: tall dark-skinned woman, 25+, long natural curls; the same high-neck,
  fully lined cobalt/red/white couture-lace knee-length dress in every scene,
  with no sheer areas.
- Boss: fictional adult man, 30+, dark tailored suit; shown mainly from behind
  or partial profile so the four established women remain the identity focus.

Only the four accepted Signal Four portraits are used as face references.
`source/keyframes/scene-02.png` is the exact wardrobe master for the complete
film; color similarity alone is not enough for continuity.

## Set and camera

NIGHTFLIGHT is an orbital black-glass casino with brushed gold arcs, a
red-and-black roulette wheel, deep-red velvet, and cyan/magenta practical
lights. Earth and a quiet launch complex are visible beyond panoramic glass.

The six clips imply one continuous left-to-right camera move. Scene boundaries
hide behind a gold pillar, roulette wheel, closing elevator door, shoulder
occlusion, or soft whip pan. Use 35 mm anamorphic language, shallow but usable
depth of field, realistic skin and fabric, and restrained film grain.

## Interaction lock

The tone is affectionate, sensual, and fashion-editorial, never explicit.
Keep everyone fully clothed. Allowed beats are warm embraces, a brief lip peck,
hands over a tailored jacket at chest and shoulders, a cheek kiss, a lingering
romantic kiss, playful jealous glances, and a shared smile. No nudity, lingerie,
sexual acts, coercion, intoxication, or fetish framing.

## Product placement

Codex Guardian is a small practical control window in the deep background for

object and no new overlay text is added.
