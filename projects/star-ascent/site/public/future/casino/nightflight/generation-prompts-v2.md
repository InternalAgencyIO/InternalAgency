# Signal Four fashion-edit record

This record covers two accepted identity-preserving wardrobe edits and one
accepted source-referenced fashion reinterpretation used by the English-only
Nightflight Casino DLC demo. The edit targets are accepted Batch 213 Signal
Four frames. All depicted people are fictional adults age 25+, fully clothed,
consensual, and non-explicit. No rejected candidate is published or represented
as an accepted asset.

The Hue signal-console frame is a direct WebP derivative of accepted Batch 213
asset 873. It retains that source's lacquered technical textile and opaque
guipure-applique wardrobe; no generated wardrobe edit is claimed for it. It is
retained as inactive provenance evidence and is not referenced by current demo
surfaces. Version 2 initially reused the accepted latex-and-lace relay frame for
the tension slot; `generation-prompts-v3.md` records the distinct frame that now
serves the active tension slot.

## Web delivery transform

The accepted PNG masters are retained under
`assets/lore/starlight-era/casino-nightflight-signal-four-v2/`. Public delivery
files are generated with Sharp 0.35.2 using a 720 by 1280 `cover` resize,
center positioning, WebP quality 84, and smart chroma subsampling. Exact source,
master, and public hashes are recorded in `asset-provenance.json`.

Four host portraits are deterministic crops of the accepted finale master,
resized to 480 by 720 WebP with the same quality and chroma-subsampling settings.
Their exact crop rectangles, hashes, and byte counts are recorded in
`asset-provenance.json`; they are delivery derivatives, not new generations.

The project-owned prompt, selection, and transformation metadata are dedicated
under CC0 1.0 Universal. The dedication applies only to rights the project can
dedicate and does not expand rights in model inputs, identities, trademarks, or
third-party material.
