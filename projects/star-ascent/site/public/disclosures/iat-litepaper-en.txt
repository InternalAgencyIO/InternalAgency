Version 1.1 — 27 July 2026

STATUS: PRE-LAUNCH DESIGN DRAFT — NOT LIVE TOKEN EVIDENCE

This document describes an intended community-token design. It does not prove
that a token exists, that any configuration is live, that an allocation is
final, or that mint or freeze authority has been changed.

1. Purpose

$IAT is proposed as a community layer for Internal Agency, a staged creative
and technical project. It does not represent equity, ownership, a deposit, a
claim on revenue, or a promise of price, yield, exchange listing, access, or
financial return.

2. Proposed token design — not verified

- Network design target: Solana
- Ticker design target: IAT
- Initial-supply design target: 1,000,000,000 IAT
- Decimal design target: 9
- Presale: none planned
- Yield, interest, or rebase: none planned at launch
- Official mint address: NOT PUBLISHED
- Mint authority state: NOT VERIFIED
- Freeze authority state: NOT VERIFIED

Every address must be treated as unofficial until the full address appears on
the official website and livestream screen and is linked to direct explorer
evidence. Plans, screenshots, names, and logos are not on-chain evidence.

3. Proposed allocation design — not final

- Community campaign and future claims: design target 50%
- Liquidity: design target 15%
- Ecosystem and product treasury: design target 15%
- Contributors: design target 10%
- Founder: design target 10%

These percentages are a proposal, not a completed allocation. Recipient
categories, token amounts, labeled public wallets, custody, vesting, lock, and
release terms remain pending. The final published map must total 100% and match
the independently verified supply.

Distribution must remain paused until the final configuration, allocation map,
authority state, release controls, and direct evidence links are public and
mutually consistent.

4. Community registration boundary

Registration is free and should request only a human-readable wallet message.
It must not request a transaction, transfer, token approval, spending
permission, seed phrase, private key, password, remote access, or payment.
Registration does not guarantee an allocation, value, product access, or any
other benefit.

5. Risk disclosure

$IAT, if launched, would be highly speculative. Digital assets can lose all
value, may be volatile or illiquid, and may be restricted by applicable law.
No price appreciation, return, liquidity, listing, or future utility is
promised.

6. Publication gate

Before any distribution, the official site must link:

- the final mint address, token program, decimals, and verified supply;
- direct explorer evidence for mint and freeze authority state;
- the complete allocation map with labeled public wallets;
- all vesting, lock, custody, and release terms; and
- a UTC verification timestamp and public distribution-reporting method.

Until then, every launch-critical field remains pending.
