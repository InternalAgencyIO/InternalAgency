STAR ASCENT // THE SIGNAL IS OPEN
Internal Agency White Dossier — Version 2.0 — Pre-launch canonical draft
Preferred public name: White Dossier.

0. TRANSMISSION

The future is not handed down. It is taken back.

STAR ASCENT is the first public transmission of Internal Agency: a living experiment in collective imagination, technology, culture, and self-determination. It is not a finished world pretending to be one. It is an opening in the wall: a place to meet, create, test, and build in public.

1. WHY THIS EXISTS

Artificial intelligence is changing the texture of daily life faster than institutions can explain it. The question is not whether systems will grow more powerful. The question is who gets to shape the worlds built around them.

Internal Agency is for people who want to participate rather than spectate. We are interested in tools, stories, rituals, creative experiments, local networks, and collective projects that increase human agency. STAR ASCENT gives that intent a public signal and a community layer.

2. THE PUBLIC CHAPTER

The launch is deliberately staged.

Genesis opens the public record: this site, the livestream, the operator registration path, and the first documents.

Distribution begins only after the complete evidence packet is public and independently checkable.

IA previews arrive when an experience is ready to be used in public—not when a roadmap needs decoration.

This is not a promise that every future idea exists now. It is a commitment to distinguish imagination, intent, and evidence.

3. $IAT — THE COMMUNITY LAYER

$IAT is the community token proposed for STAR ASCENT on Solana. It is a signal and a gathering mechanism, not a claim on revenue, equity, profit, or guaranteed return. There is no presale, no paid registration, and no request for money from the audience.

Holding, participating, or associating with the project does not create a promise of price, yield, APY, or profit. Exchanges and independent parties make their own decisions; Internal Agency does not represent them and does not control their conduct.

4. TOKEN DESIGN

The intended maximum supply is 1,000,000,000 IAT. The complete allocation and release model is in the canonical Tokenomics document.

50% community distribution
20% treasury / long-term build
15% ecosystem / collaborators
10% core team
5% liquidity / launch operations

The initial mint is followed by permanent revocation of mint and freeze authority. Non-circulating allocations are held in publicly verifiable time-lock wallets. Each allocation, lock address, release term, and circulation calculation must be public before distribution begins.

5. THE EVIDENCE RULE

An announcement is not proof. Before any public distribution, STAR ASCENT must publish:

- final mint address, program, decimals, supply, and UTC timestamp;
- explorer evidence of mint and freeze authority status;
- public allocation wallets, amounts, percentages, locks, and release schedules;
- the method for calculating circulating supply; and
- the official channel record linking the site, pinned announcement, and livestream.

If these facts are missing or conflict, the correct state is HOLD. Do not rely on urgency, direct messages, screenshots, or copied addresses.

6. OPERATOR ETHOS

An operator is not a customer record. An operator is a participant who verifies information, brings their own judgment, and helps make the network more legible.

Registration, when opened, asks only for an understandable wallet-message signature. It never asks for a seed phrase, private key, password, or payment. If a wallet prompt requests a transfer, token approval, spending permission, or recovery phrase, stop.

7. ASSOCIATE DLC

One week after genesis, Associate DLC is intended to introduce a non-custodial affiliation and vouching layer. It is a social and organizational primitive: an associate may publicly support a node or mission without giving up wallet custody.

Associate DLC has no fixed financial reward, APY, or implied return. Any future incentive design must be introduced through a separate public proposal that states its funding source, formula, eligibility, duration, governance process, and risks. Until then, affiliation is affiliation—not an investment contract or reward claim.

8. WHAT COMES NEXT

STAR ASCENT will be judged by what it makes visible: experiments, public artifacts, collaborators, evidence, and the quality of its community. The project will choose the strange, the beautiful, and the useful over empty certainty.

The signal is open. Build with discernment.
