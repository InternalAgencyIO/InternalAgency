STAR ASCENT — ENGLISH LAUNCH REHEARSAL PLAYBOOK
PRE-LAUNCH DRAFT — A REHEARSAL IS NOT LAUNCH APPROVAL
Revision: 2026-07-27

PURPOSE

This playbook helps the launch team rehearse public communication and safety
controls. It does not authorize minting, deployment, signing, broadcasting,
distribution, wallet transactions, or publication of a token address.

Every unresolved fact stays PENDING. A scheduled time never overrides a safety
hold. No operator should handle a seed phrase, private key, password, personal
data, payment, or community member's funds.

REHEARSAL ROLES

- Show caller: advances only approved cues and can pause the entire run.
- Evidence reviewer: compares every launch-critical field with direct public
  evidence and records PASS, HOLD, or FAIL.
- Stream operator: shows only the approved screen set; never improvises a token
  address, wallet, chart, price, capacity number, or countdown promise.
- Community lead: answers from the approved bilingual scripts and routes
  unresolved questions without guessing.
- Safety observer: watches for scam reports, unexpected wallet requests,
  mismatched domains, inaccessible disclosures, and misleading language.

No single role can clear its own disputed evidence. The show caller and evidence
reviewer must agree before a held cue can resume.

PAUSE AND ESCALATION MATRIX

LEVEL 0 — ROUTINE QUESTION

Examples: schedule clarification, document location, registration timing.
Action: answer from the approved script; link only to the official site.
Resume rule: no pause is required unless the question exposes a mismatch.

LEVEL 1 — CONTENT MISMATCH

Examples: English and Turkish copy differ; a time, domain, status, or document
revision is inconsistent.
Action: stop the affected post or screen; display the neutral HOLD message;
route the mismatch to the show caller and evidence reviewer.
Resume rule: two-person comparison passes and every public surface is corrected.

LEVEL 2 — WALLET OR ADDRESS WARNING

Examples: unexpected transaction request, spending permission, token approval,
address from a reply or DM, mismatched address character, fake support wallet.
Action: stop all wallet demonstrations and address publication; tell the
community not to transact; preserve only non-sensitive evidence of the report;
route to the safety observer and evidence reviewer.
Resume rule: the warning is explained, affected surfaces are removed, and the
full human evidence gate is repeated. Time pressure is never a resume reason.

LEVEL 3 — SECRET, PAYMENT, OR IMPERSONATION INCIDENT

Examples: request for a seed phrase, private key, password, payment, remote
access, or personal data; compromised official account; fraudulent domain.
Action: stop the launch disclosure and all wallet activity; publish the approved
bilingual pause message on uncompromised official surfaces; do not ask the
reporter to send secrets or funds; escalate to the responsible account-security
owner outside public chat.
Resume rule: no same-session assumption. Resume only after account ownership,
public surfaces, evidence, and communications are independently revalidated.

APPROVED HOLD MESSAGE

STAR ASCENT remains paused while we verify launch-critical information. Do not
use addresses or links from replies or direct messages, do not take wallet
action, and never share a seed phrase, private key, password, or payment. The
official site will show the next verified status.
